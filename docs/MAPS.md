# Maps

All maps are listed in `scripts/maps.gd`.

- **Townhouses**: two rows of houses with upstairs rooms, a street with a bus, backyards and side flanks. Three pickups stand in the plaza (#144): Extra Messen and a Herstelkern either side of the bus (x ±8) and a Schildplaat on the bus lane at z 11, all 1.2 m touch radius, respawning after 20 / 22 / 28 s. The mid plaza (about 52 m of open floor, covered by the bus and a few crates) has a wallrun alley on each side: two 14 m faces 7.4 m apart at 3.4 m, one west of the street and one east, so crossing the plaza has a route that is not a 50 m sprint in the open. `check_townhouses` asserts the faces are real wallrun surfaces — a collider whose normal is flatter than `Player.WALLRUN_MAX_NY` at chest height — not just navmesh holes; that assert caught the west alley's inner wall being dropped from the scene.
- **Foundry**: pickups: Extra Messen in the west container yard, Herstelkern in the east courtyard, Schildplaat in the hall. A compact industrial yard. Roofed warehouse hall in the middle (skylight, big doors north and south, side doors east) with a 3 m mezzanine that looks out over the container yard. Walk ramps just west of the big doors reach the 7.3 m roof; a bridge crosses the skylight to the east half, and two 18 m wallrun walls run along the skylight edges (split at the bridge, so the crossing stays open), which also makes the skylight edge safe while the outer roof edge stays a deliberate drop. Surf ramps run along the hall's west wall, one each side of the container. Container hill and stacks on the west side; an alley with offset gates, two sheds with three doors each, and a water-tank courtyard on the east side; loading docks behind both spawns. No spawn sees the other spawn, and the long alley is broken up so the sniper has lanes but no lane covers the whole map.
- **Rooftops** (v2, `tools/gen_rooftops.py`): about 70 × 100 m, outer walls 12.5 m. Two brick blocks (x ±16, z ±16..34) with 6 m roofs around a 32 m court, and two skybridges at roof height (x ±12..16). Laid out around flow lines. Each team owns one flank surf: off its own roof along a launch deck at 6 m, then a 48 m surf down the outer wall (6 → 0 m, 7°) into the other team's back lane, with a strip halfway and one at the foot (LINE ×2); Blue rides the west wall toward −Z, Orange the east wall toward +Z. From each roof edge a court surf (a smaller 57° section, 5 → 0 m over 18 m) drops to the middle of the court onto a strip, and its run-out goes straight up the other team's court ramp onto their roof; the surf's filled back is a vertical court wall to wallrun. A steel gantry spans the court at 11 m as the grapple anchor. Each flank is split by a 14 m, 3.4 m tall wall (wallrun on both faces) and truck-height blocks; lane breakers are 1.90 m or taller, because 1.40 is cover the eye sees over. Yards behind a team-coloured spawn wall and two baffles, with two 2.6 m containers each. Pickups: Extra Messen and Herstelkern in the flank lanes (x ±28), Schildplaat in the middle of the court. `check_rooftops` bakes the navmesh (roofs, decks, bridges, ramps, flanks, court), rides both flank and court surfs with a real pawn feathering strafe (each has to reach strip speed and land in the other half), measures every ground lane at eye height (35 m max) and asserts no blue spawn sees an orange one (144 pairs).
- **Quay**: pickups: Extra Messen and Herstelkern on the two quay decks (x ±30, y 4), Schildplaat on the dock floor at z 18. A dry dock, about 90 × 120 m. The dock floor is at 0 m, the side quays at 4 m, and the loods roofs at 10 m. The flank surf starts on the roof: run west along the south edge, onto a pad, and a banked quarter turn puts you on the outer wall. It runs down the wall past the quay deck and on to the floor of the far lane, about 75 m in all. There is a speed strip where it passes the end of the deck and one at its foot, so one clean ride scores LINE ×2 and drops you in the other team's back lane. Two container stacks beside it give the rider some cover. The corner surf does not end in the air any more: beside its run-out a second surf takes over (the two make a V), runs west across the dock, banks through a quarter turn and becomes the surf on the quay wall, which ends on its own speed strip. Corner strip then dock strip, without touching the floor, scores LINE ×2. A 4 m underpass under that connector keeps the dock floor in one piece. The middle of the dock under the gantry is open floor. On the east roof a balcony feeds a quarter-circle surf (14 m radius, short straight segments, with a straight run-in and run-out) that turns west into the dock, toward the middle of the map. The orange corner turns east into the dock. A steel walk at the north-east corner of each loods climbs from the quay to the roof, about 24°, so you can reach that corner again. Stairs inside each loods and a narrow street ramp up to the roof are the other ways up, with the foot on open floor. Bots take those, not the surf. Each quay deck runs out to the outer wall, so the side lanes have no dead end: under the run-in pad, up a 12° walk ramp onto the deck, and down the gutter beside the lower flank surf into the far lane. On each quay stands a two-floor harbour office you can walk through (doors west, north and south, a stair to the top floor, an opening to drop back onto the deck). A crane gantry spans the dock between the two offices with a container hanging under it, well above every surf. Containers (two high in places) are the only loose cover in the dock and on the decks. The roof edge facing the enemy has a 2.4 m screen with two sniper slits; the balcony has a screen too. Spawns are 2 × 6 per team in the yard behind the spawn wall; the yard corners are solid.
- **Skyline**: pickups: Extra Messen and Herstelkern on the low west/east roofs (y 4, x ∓25), Schildplaat at the mid roof edge (5, 6, -6). City rooftops from 4 to 15 m over a street far below; a fall under the roofs is a death. Roofs are 12–15 m across, and every connector between them is a 5–6 m wide ramp deck (≤ 37°), so a lane is one surface you can sprint and slide: west is low and stepped (9 → 6 → 4), east is high (15 fire escape, 9, 4), the centre is a straight 30 m spine from the back of the spawn roof over a 5.2 m bridge to the tower roof, which ends along an 8.2 m steel wall for a wallrun. The tower is hollow (room at 6–11 m, stairwell, roof hatch) and holds the Mid bridge. A 7.6 m wide wallrun alley with two 13 m faces sits on the east roof. The surf street is a 10 m wide ridge running 20 m down a 9.6° pitch between the west lane and the tower: enter from the drop-in pad beside the crest (reached on foot from WA over an 11° link), surf the ridge, and land on the SurfDeck at 6 m. Three catch ledges — under the centre bridge, and the lower lane reaching into its gap on both sides — turn a missed jump into lost height instead of a death. No spawn sees the other spawn, and `check_skyline` asserts that on all 144 spawn pairs at eye height: the front baffles reach past |x| = 13.6 (they stopped at 10, which left the outer spawn columns looking straight across the map) and the tower-roof sign is 2.2 m wide (1.6 m let the inner columns pass it by 10 cm).
