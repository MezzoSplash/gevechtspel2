# Changelog

Release notes, newest first. Downloads are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/MezzoSplash/gevechtspel2/releases).

**v0.3.10** — New map Skyline (city rooftops with height, a surf ramp over the street gap, no more snag on the EastStair) and a fall icon in the kill feed. New guns: LMG and Slug Shotgun. Settings get tabs, key rebinding, crosshair, audio buses and accessibility options. The Tab scoreboard is redesigned with two team panels and K, D and K/D columns. The join menu remembers your last 5 servers. Streak slots, weapon slots, the health bar (green, yellow, red) and class select have a new look. **Not compatible with v0.3.8 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.3.10): everyone must update, including the dedicated server.

**v0.3.8** — New main menu and settings with a shared theme: hover and keyboard/pad focus move together and Esc steps back a screen. Two players can no longer share a name: the server makes it "Name (2)", "(3)" and so on, and an empty name becomes "Player". Dead code and unused assets are gone. **Compatible with v0.3.7** (NET_VERSION stays 0.3.7).

**v0.3.7** — The RC-XD has weight: it accelerates, brakes before it reverses, steers toward the mouse, slides a little in a hard turn, keeps its speed off a kicker, and bounces on landings and walls. Downhill ramps no longer crawl. The blast kills out to about 4.4 m at the chest, the camera stays on the explosion for a second, and the car has its own boom plus a gear whine while you drive. Frags and the RC-XD shove players and cars. Deaths tumble, guns sway (Settings → Weapon sway: Off, Reduced, Full), and shells eject. The 3-kill streak is a 25-second UAV on a minimap at the top left; a dot fades for a second after you lose them. Without a UAV, a shot still marks that spot. **Not compatible with v0.3.5 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.3.7): everyone must update, including the dedicated server.

**v0.3.5** — Quay looks better. An overcast sky lights the map, with AgX tonemapping, soft four-split sun shadows, light SSAO, glow only on muzzle flashes and tracers, and light distance fog from about 35 m that never hides a player. Walls and floors get procedural painted wear and simple patterns (brick, concrete seams, container ribs, roof sheets, asphalt). New Settings → Graphics: Low, Medium or High; Low turns off SSAO, glow, fog and wall detail for the most FPS. Graphics only: same NET_VERSION 0.3.4 as v0.3.4, so v0.3.4 and v0.3.5 can play together. Not compatible with v0.3.3 or older.

**v0.3.4** — Quay is rebuilt. Side lanes run out to the outer wall. Each quay has a two-floor harbour office, a crane spans the dock with a hanging container, and the roof facing the enemy has a 2.4 m screen with two sniper slits. The flank surf starts on the roof, banks through a quarter turn, and runs down the outer wall into the far lane; one clean ride scores LINE ×2. The corner surf hands off into the dock surf for a second LINE ×2, and the middle of the dock is open floor. The SMG has a new viewmodel with a fire animation and muzzle flash. The RC-XD is the RCExplody car, and earning it plays "RC-XD ready for deployment." On the ground you step up lips to 0.32 m. **Not compatible with v0.3.3 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.3.4): everyone must update, including the dedicated server.

**v0.3.3** — Quay cleanup. Doors that looked shut are open, the west wall of each loods has a shoot hole into the stair, and the stair crests no longer have a small step. Floating blocks on the edges are gone. The steel walk is unchanged. **Not compatible with v0.3.2 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.3.3): everyone must update, including the dedicated server.

**v0.3.2** — Surfing a ramp keeps your speed for 4 seconds. A yellow strip at the end of the surfs on Quay, Foundry and Rooftops scores LINE (+100) if you cross it fast, and LINE ×2 (+200) for a second strip in that window. Your team, including you, hears "Friendly radar online"; the other team hears "Enemy radar online". Same NET_VERSION 0.3.0 as v0.3.0 and v0.3.1, so those builds can still join. Not compatible with 0.2.22 or older.

**v0.3.1** — Revolver cylinder and grip sit on the gun. The streak counter stays `STREAK n/5` during radar and while driving the RC-XD. An unused killstreak clears when the next round starts. Every map has 12 team spawns, so a full bot match does not stack on one point. Discord shows that you are playing while the window is open. Same NET_VERSION 0.3.0 as v0.3.0, so 0.3.0 and 0.3.1 can still join each other. Not compatible with 0.2.22 or older.

**v0.3.0** — New map Quay: a dry dock with downhill surfs, a curved corner into the dock, and a steel walk from the quay up to the roof so you can take that corner again. **Not compatible with 0.2.22 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.3.0): everyone must update, including the dedicated server. Release builds are Windows x86_64 and Linux x86_64 only.

**v0.2.22** — Solo and host pick the kill limit, round length and bot count next to map and mode. Esc → Settings no longer crashes mid-match. SMG damage is 10 and falls off from 12 m; the rifle is 34 damage at 390 rpm with a headshot kill out to about 27 m; the revolver needs two headshots; the shotgun is 32 × 12 pellets; the sniper draws no crosshair until you zoom. **Not compatible with 0.2.21 or older** (NET_VERSION 0.2.22): everyone must update, including the dedicated server.

**v0.2.21** — Earned killstreaks stay until you use them. Radar unlocks at 3 kills in one life; RC-XD at 5 (drive the car from its own camera and detonate it). Bots commit to one strafe and flank instead of shimmying; SMG bots burst shorter. Settings save the window, resolution, VSync, FPS cap, FOV, render scale, antialiasing, invert Y and an FPS counter.
