**VERIFY** = origin or license not documented; replace or get it in writing before release.

## Required notices in a shipped build

- Godot Engine MIT license and its third-party notices (Engine.get_license_text / `COPYRIGHT.txt`).
- MIT notice for discord-rich-presence-godot (`addons/discord_rich_presence/LICENSE`).
- Piper voice credit and MIT notice, as in CREDITS.md (courtesy; the clips are released CC0).
- CC0 packs need no credit; keep the CREDITS.md lines anyway.

## Build hygiene

`export_presets.cfg` uses `export_filter="all_resources"` and only excludes `testmodels/*`, so `docs/` (moodboard JPGs, PR screenshots) and `tools/` are packed too. Add `docs/*, tools/*` to `exclude_filter` before a release build.
